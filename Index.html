Purchasing Portal help

            

            

               
    
                  
  • Use New request to ask the ordering team to place a purchase. Check the charging department and delivery site before submitting.
    • 
                  
  • The budget’s assigned processors are emailed about new requests submitted by other people. You do not receive this notification for your own submission.
    • 
                  
  • Use Requested for me or Submitted by me in Order filters to track your own requests.
    • 
                  
  • Open a request to check its status, delivery estimate or notes. Use Notes to ask a question while a request is pending or after it has been ordered.
    • 
                  
  • With purchasing permissions, use Already ordered to record a purchase placed with the supplier. Use Log invoice / direct spend for a separate invoice or credit note.
